Lazio's third airport

Latina Airport, ready to open to civil aviation.

An already-existing airport site, connected to Rome in 35 minutes by Intercity train, that can become Lazio's third airport — to relieve Fiumicino and Ciampino and serve the southern Pontine area, the lower Lazio region and the islands.

The Committee's action

Our observation to the public consultation

The Committee has submitted a formal observation to the SEA consultation on the National Airports Plan 2026-2035 (procedure no. 8657, Italian Ministry for the Environment — MASE): we ask that Lazio's third airport be chosen through a transparent technical comparison in which Latina is assessed on its full merits.

What we ask

  • That the SEA identify, assess and compare the reasonable alternatives for the location — Latina, Frosinone, Viterbo, the zero option — as required by Directive 2001/42/EC.
  • A comparative feasibility study, on an equal-budget basis and with 100% of the technical work made public, with the results themselves put out to consultation.
  • Recognition that Latina is today the best choice for intermodality (~40 min from Rome, an already-existing site, lower environmental impact) — available now and without major new works.

Who it is addressed to

Recipient: MASE — Environmental Assessments Directorate / EIA-SEA Commission (SEA procedure no. 8657).

Copied to: ENAC, MIT, ENAV, the Air Force, the Lazio Region, the City and Province of Latina, the Transport Committees of the Chamber and the Senate, the Frosinone-Latina Chamber of Commerce.

Why Latina Airport

The reasons, consistent with the Plan's goals

Why the Pontine airport is the most rational choice for Lazio's third airport.

1
An already-existing infrastructure. The "Comani" site and runway are already there: lower costs and shorter timelines than a brand-new airport.
2
Connected to Rome in 35 minutes. Latina Scalo station is on the Rome–Naples line: rail-air intermodality, a key objective of the Plan.
3
Relieves Fiumicino and Ciampino. Ciampino has operational and environmental constraints; a third airport eases the Rome system.
4
A large catchment area. The Province of Latina, lower Lazio and the islands: a populous area today without a nearby airport.
5
Tourism. Circeo, Sperlonga, Gaeta, Ponza and Ventotene: significant, seasonal tourist demand.
6
Integrated networks. Integrated airport systems and intermodality are cornerstone principles of the new National Plan.

These arguments are developed in the document the Committee submitted to ENAC's public consultation.

Mission

Building the historical memory of Lazio's third airport

The Committee was formed to build a complete knowledge base of every act, fact, statement, document, parliamentary question, opinion and stakeholder relating to Lazio's third airport, with a focus on Latina.

A public, verifiable and open resource, so that the debate rests on facts and not on hearsay. You'll find it in the Research and Documentary Archive section.

About us

A citizens' committee, informal and non-partisan

We are a currently informal committee of citizens who want to bring transparency and shared knowledge and historical memory, so that the Pontine airport infrastructure can open to civil aviation.

We work by collecting and organising documents and facts, and making them available to everyone in an open and verifiable way.

Non-partisan

Neutrality and civic spirit

The Committee does not represent any party or political side. It is a civic initiative: anyone who shares the goal can contribute, regardless of political affiliation.
